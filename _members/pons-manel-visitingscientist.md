Manel is a PhD student in the lab of David de Gonzalo-Calvo at the Lleida Institute of Biomedical Research in Lleida, Spain. He works on the identification of host and viral miRNAs that may act as markers for clinical disease severity.

He was awarded an EMBO Scientific Exchange Grant to join the Depledge lab for three months to gain experience in the identification and analysis of herpesvirus miRNAs and to learn how to build analytical 
pipelines for both host and viral RNA analysis.
