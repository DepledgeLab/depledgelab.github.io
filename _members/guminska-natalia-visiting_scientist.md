Natalia is a postdoc in the lab of Andrzej Dziembowski at the International Institute of Molecular and Cell Biology in Warsaw, Poland. She recently developed the tool [Ninetails](https://github.com/LRB-IIMCB/ninetails) which enables measurements of non-A nucleotide incorporations into poly(A) tails and has been used extensively by our lab. She received an EMBO Scientific Exchange Grant to join the Depledge lab for three months where she has continued with the development of ninetails, its successor, and a new tool, Lachesis.
