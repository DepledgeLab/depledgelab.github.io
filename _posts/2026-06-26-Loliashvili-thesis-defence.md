Today, Elene became the second PhD student to graduate from the Depledge Lab (and the first from the MHH lab)! She successfully defended her thesis entitled 'Epitranscriptomics Regulation of Varicella Zoster Virus Infection'.

Congratulations to Elene and a big thank you to the external examiners Redmond Smyth and Luca Cicin-Sain. Thank you also to the thesis advisory committee members Abel Viejo-Borbolla and Benjamin Nilsson-Payant. 

Best of all, Elene won a prize for having the best thesis!
